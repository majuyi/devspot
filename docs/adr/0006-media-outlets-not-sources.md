# 0006 · Media outlets are discovery inputs, not sources

**Context.** TechNG scraped TechCabal and Techpoint. They report on opportunities after the fact and mix them with news.

**Decision.** Do not poll media. Use it during registry research to find organizations.

**Consequences.** No news classifier needed in the pipeline; coverage sits upstream at the source.

**Status.** Accepted, 2026-09-06.
