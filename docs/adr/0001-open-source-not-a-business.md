# 0001 · Open source, not a business

**Context.** The original framing (from a ChatGPT conversation) treated this as a company with three products. Dami's intent is an open-source project like CLIST.

**Decision.** Open source under a permissive licence. No ads, paid listings or accounts to monetise. Sustainability is continuity, not revenue.

**Consequences.** Removes monetization pressure. Adds maintainer-load risk: CLIST shows 93% of commits stay with one person even with every contribution affordance. Every design choice must minimise per-source maintenance.

**Status.** Accepted, 2026-09-06.
