# 0002 · The unit of ingestion is the organization

**Context.** Opportunities scatter across WhatsApp, X, newsletters and boards, but each originates from one organization publishing on one page.

**Decision.** The registry lists organizations and their sources. The engine polls sources; it never crawls the open web. X and media are used by a human to discover organizations.

**Consequences.** The problem is bounded and enumerable. Coverage grows by adding YAML files. Informal-channel items arrive through submissions, not scraping.

**Status.** Accepted, 2026-09-06.
